   - In production, functions like `_seleccionar_archivo`, `_seleccionar_ruta_salida`, and `_ejecutar` call Tkinter native modals (`filedialog` and `messagebox`) as well as `os.startfile(out_path)`.

It then reads headers from row 5, which in non-Clemont sheets is often blank or data.
